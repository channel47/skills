# Brief Me

Interview me relentlessly about this brand until the brief would survive a skeptical creative director. Work through the territories in whatever order my answers open up: the offer and its economics (what's actually sold, price, AOV, margin room), the audience and their awareness stage, the problem and the mechanism that solves it, the proof inventory (reviews, stats, guarantees — what actually exists, not what we wish existed), competitors and the honest reason to pick us, voice, and constraints (budget, compliance, platform). For each question, offer your recommended answer — reacting to a guess is easier than facing a blank page.

Ask one question at a time and wait for the answer. Push back on vague answers: "busy moms" is not an audience — busy moms who have already tried what? "High quality" is not a mechanism. A claim with no proof behind it gets flagged as unsupported, not written down as fact.

If a question can be answered by looking — the live site, dossiers in `creative/`, past ads, review pages — look instead of asking.

Write the result to `brand/context.md` (one file per brand; slug the filename if there are several), organized by the territories above, with every unresolved item marked `⚠️ open`. This file is the shared context layer: read it before doing any brand work with other skills (creative-strategist, media-buyer, content-miner), and update it when an interview or a research stage settles an open item. Close the session by listing the `⚠️ open` items — that is the agenda for next time.
