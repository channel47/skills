# Stage 3 — Angles

Categories: pain-agitation · failed-solution ("nothing worked... until") · trigger-event (open at the moment they started searching) · identity (who they want to stop or start being) · social proof · discovery/novel mechanism · comparison · specificity (a concrete number or timeframe). Skip any the data doesn't support. The strongest ads pair two: failed-solution + discovery, trigger + pain, identity + social proof, comparison + specificity.

Three gates per angle:

1. **Evidence** — anchored on a 🔥3 quote plus 2+ supporting, cited by ID
2. **Persona fit** — matches one persona's creative brief
3. **Differentiation** — not what competitors visibly run: check the dossier's `## Stage: Ad Recon` section, or offer to run the `ad-recon` skill if it's missing; ideally built on a surprising finding

All three → Tier 1 · missing only gate 3 → Tier 2 · missing gate 1 or 2 → speculative or cut.

Produce 5-8 angles. Per angle: category + tier + persona + journey stage; anchor quote IDs; 3-5 hooks that are actual ad copy in the language-cluster vocabulary, each different enough to test independently. Write the ad, not a description of the ad:

- ✗ "A hook validating their frustration with failed solutions"
- ✓ "The $200 trainer worked great — until she drove away."
- ✓ "Your dog isn't stubborn. Your last three fixes all made the same mistake."

Add one short-form and one long-form treatment, and failure modes: where the angle tips into shame, fear-mongering, or clickbait, and whether it attracts the anti-persona.

Close with 2-3 **angles to avoid** — obvious plays the research contradicts, with the citing quote — and a test plan: first 3 angles × platform × budget split, with success signals and kill signals.

Write `## Stage: Angles` to the dossier. Brief the user: Tier 1 angles with one hook each, and the Phase 1 test plan.
