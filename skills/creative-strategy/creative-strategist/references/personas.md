# Stage 2 — Personas

Cluster the research quotes by behavior, not demographics — demographics describe people, behavior predicts response to ads. Cluster on: journey entry point; prior-solution history (naive first-timer vs. burned veteran — usually the sharpest split); conviction pattern (what evidence converts them: peers, data, authority, risk-reversal). Merge clusters that would respond to the same ad; split where one ad would fail half the group. 2-4 personas — if two share the same hook, proof, and CTA, they're one persona.

Each persona, citing quote IDs throughout:

- **Name that encodes the tension** — "The Burned Buyer", never "Sarah, 34"
- Snapshot: entry point, solution history, demographic signals only where quotes support them
- First-person decision monologue tracing the arc — trigger → search → compare → hesitate → what would tip them. Calibration excerpt:
  > "My neighbor left a note about the barking. Again. I've done the YouTube methods, the $200 trainer, a citronella collar — each worked for about a week (Q7, Q12, Q31). So I'm googling reviews at midnight, but honestly I don't believe any of these things work anymore. Show me someone whose dog was as bad as mine (Q18)."
- Top pains by intensity; stated vs. deeper desire; objections + what overcomes each; what they've already tried and why each failed
- Language fingerprint: their verbatim phrases, grouped by emotional register
- Creative brief — one concrete line each for **Lead with / Prove with / Avoid / CTA style / Best platform / Hook archetype**. Concrete means executable: "before/after videos from owners who also 'tried everything', plus a 30-day refund" — not "social proof".

Close with one **anti-persona**: who will never convert for structural reasons (opposed philosophy, wrong category, perpetual researcher), how to recognize them in targeting, evidence quote. This is where ad spend goes to die.

Write `## Stage: Personas` to the dossier. Brief the user: persona names with one-line tensions, the anti-persona, and the sharpest behavioral split.
