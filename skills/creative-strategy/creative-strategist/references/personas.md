# Stage 2 — Personas

Cluster the research quotes by behavior, not demographics — demographics describe people, while observed behavior more directly informs creative choices. Cluster on: journey entry point; prior-solution history; conviction pattern (what evidence they say they trust: peers, data, authority, risk-reversal). Merge clusters that call for the same hook, proof, and CTA; split clusters when the cited evidence points to different creative needs. Produce 2-4 personas.

Each persona, citing quote IDs throughout:

- **Name that encodes the tension** — "The Burned Buyer", never "Sarah, 34"
- Snapshot: entry point, solution history, demographic signals only where quotes support them
- First-person decision monologue tracing the arc — trigger → search → compare
  → hesitate → what would tip them. Label every generated monologue
  `Synthetic composite—not a customer quote` and cite the quote IDs used.
  Illustrative format (synthetic):
  > "My neighbor left a note about the barking. Again. I've done the YouTube methods, the $200 trainer, a citronella collar — each worked for about a week (Q7, Q12, Q31). So I'm googling reviews at midnight, but honestly I don't believe any of these things work anymore. Show me someone whose dog was as bad as mine (Q18)."
- Top pains by intensity; stated vs. deeper desire; objections + what overcomes each; what they've already tried and why each failed
- Language fingerprint: their verbatim phrases, grouped by emotional register
- Creative brief — one concrete line each for **Lead with / Prove with / Avoid
  / CTA style / Best platform / Hook archetype**. Concrete means executable:
  `"before/after videos supported by Q7 and Q12, plus [confirmed guarantee]"`
  — not `"social proof"`.

Close with one **anti-persona**: a group the evidence suggests is unlikely to convert for structural reasons (opposed philosophy, wrong category, perpetual researcher), how to recognize them in targeting, and the supporting quote IDs.

Write `## Stage: Personas` to the dossier. Brief the user: persona names with one-line tensions, the anti-persona, and the sharpest behavioral split.
