# Stage 4 — Advertorial

Turn the chosen angle (default: top Tier 1) into a build-ready presell page. The page earns the sale before the offer appears, moving the reader: recognition → stakes → mechanism → proof → objections answered → bridge to product. Pick the lightest archetype that fits: problem-solution, honest review, listicle, comparison, myth-busting, founder story.

Write the full copy in the persona's language fingerprint: subheads that carry the story for skimmers, short mobile paragraphs, strongest proof in the first third, biggest objection answered before the final CTA, CTA as the natural next step rather than a hard pitch.

End with two blocks:

**Proof ledger** — every meaningful claim rated proven / needs-source / demo-only / remove, each with publish-safe wording:

| Claim | Status | Safe wording |
|---|---|---|
| "Works for 9 out of 10 dogs" | needs-source | Cut, or replace with the Q22 testimonial until a stat exists |
| "Vets recommend ultrasonic training" | remove | No source in research — do not publish |

Use placeholders like `[needs testimonial]` rather than inventing proof. In regulated categories (health, finance, kids), downgrade claims to observable facts.

**Build notes** — images needed, CTA destination and tracking, ad-to-page message match, one A/B variant worth testing.

Write `## Stage: Advertorial` to the dossier. Brief the user: archetype + angle chosen, proof-ledger status (how many claims need sources), and the next build step.
